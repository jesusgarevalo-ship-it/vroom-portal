BRAND IDENTITY

The VROOM Mark

A stylized "V" formed by two speed lanes converging — like a road rushing toward you. The negative space creates a forward-pointing arrow, symbolizing motion, progress, and velocity.

Design Rationale

The symbol works at 16px (app icon) and 10 meters (billboard). VOLT BLUE primary, IGNITION YELLOW accent. Clean, ownable, unmistakably automotive.
